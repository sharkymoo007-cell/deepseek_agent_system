# 🗓️ Optimized Health & Work Timetable

> A balanced weekly schedule designed to maximize **productivity** while protecting **physical health, mental well-being, and sleep quality**.
> Time blocks are organized around energy peaks, regular movement, hydration, and recovery.

---

## ⚙️ Optimization Principles Applied

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Deep work at peak energy** | High-focus tasks scheduled in the morning (highest cognitive capacity). |
| **Pomodoro-style focus** | 50-min focused blocks followed by 10-min movement breaks. |
| **Movement every hour** | Stand, stretch, or walk during each break to counter sedentary risk. |
| **Screen detox windows** | Regular breaks away from screens; no devices 60 min before bed. |
| **Protected recovery** | Fixed meal times, lunch walk, and a consistent sleep window (≥7.5 h). |
| **Weekly buffer** | Friday afternoon reserved for review, planning, and light admin. |

## ✅ Daily Non-Negotiables Checklist

- [ ] 7–8 hours of sleep (consistent bedtime & wake time)
- [ ] 2 L+ water intake
- [ ] 30+ minutes of exercise / movement
- [ ] 3 balanced meals + healthy snacks
- [ ] 4 deep-work blocks completed
- [ ] Hourly posture & eye breaks
- [ ] 60 min screen-free wind-down before bed

---

## 📌 Notes

- **Adjust flexibly:** Swap Deep Work Blocks with meetings based on daily reality; never sacrifice the **sleep window** or **exercise**.
- **Batch communication:** Check emails/messages only at scheduled shallow-work slots to protect focus.
- **Energy tracking:** Notice personal energy peaks and shift deep work to your strongest 2–3 hour window if needed.
- **Review weekly:** Spend 15 minutes each Friday to refine this timetable based on what worked.
